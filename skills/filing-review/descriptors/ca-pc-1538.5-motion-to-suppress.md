## Conditional / contextual rules (free-form, LLM-evaluated)

- For warrantless searches: the declaration of counsel should establish the search was warrantless. That is the trigger fact, not the legal argument. The burden then shifts to the prosecution at the hearing. [severity: warn]
- For Franks challenges (warrant cases): the declaration must plead specific false statements in the affidavit with offer of proof — substantial preliminary showing standard. Flag if the declaration is only argumentative. [severity: warn]
- Filed at the preliminary hearing rather than pretrial: the package is often abbreviated — notice and motion, oral argument, no separate memo. Adjust expectations.
- Some counties require a meet-and-confer declaration before filing certain motions; check local criminal rules. [severity: info]

## Assembly order

1. Notice of Motion and Motion to Suppress (caption-page front)
2. Memorandum of Points and Authorities
3. Declaration of counsel
4. Declaration of defendant (if applicable)
5. Exhibits (A, B, C ...)
6. Proposed Order
7. Proof of Service (typically last; some courts want first)

## Filing destination

Criminal clerk of the assigned superior court. Service on the assigned DDA (or office of the DA if not yet assigned). Most counties accept e-filing via TrueFiling, OneLegal, or a county portal — verify per county.
